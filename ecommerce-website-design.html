03Questions

Questions before we start.

How much does a custom online store cost?

You get the exact price in writing before you pay. Once accepted, that price won't change for the agreed work. We'll quote any extra requests before starting them. It's mostly down to the number of products and options, and the payment and shipping setup you'd like.

Do you take a cut of my sales?

No. Payments run on Stripe and the money goes straight to your account. Stripe charges its own card fee on each order; we don't add anything on top.

I already have a store. Can you redesign it?

Yes. We confirm compatibility, access and the migration scope first. We prepare the redesign alongside the existing store and agree the launch with you.

I already sell on Shopify. Do I have to leave it?

No. If you want your orders, stock and customers to stay in Shopify, we check your plan and setup first and, where it fits, Shopify keeps handling checkout. If you'd rather not pay for Shopify, the store runs on Stripe instead and the money goes straight to your account.

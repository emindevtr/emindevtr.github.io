01What it takes

What a small hotel site has to get right.

The place first

Open on the view, the valley or the street outside, not a room list, because that's what they're paying for. The rooms come second.

Rooms that read in seconds

Each room with its photos, size, beds and view, easy to compare on a small screen without opening five tabs.

When to come, and how

Seasons, what's on nearby and how to get there from the airport or the station, so guests don't have to email you to ask.

Book direct

A "Check dates" button on every screen that goes straight to your booking engine. We'll check that it supports direct bookings and explain any provider fees before quoting.

Fast on a phone

Most guests find you from a phone, often on the road. Light, hand-written pages open before they give up and go back to the big booking sites.

Current every season

Rates, seasonal photos, a new offer: you send them and we put them up, each one priced before we start.

02Questions

Questions before we start.

How much does a boutique hotel website cost?

You get the exact price in writing before you pay. Once accepted, that price won't change for the agreed work. We'll quote any extra requests before starting them. It's mostly down to the number of rooms and pages, and the booking engine it connects to.

Do you build the booking engine?

No. We connect the one you already use, so the "Check dates" button goes straight to it and your availability stays in a single calendar.

Who owns the site?

You do. Once you pay the agreed project fee, the website design and code are yours. You can take your site with you.

02What it takes

What a high-ticket product site has to get right.

Detail you can get close to

You'll see the clasp, dial and finish in sharp close-up images. They stay clear when buyers zoom in to inspect the details.

The craft, step by step

ÉPINE walks through its making in order: forge, weld, test, eleven days per watch. Showing the hours is how a price starts to make sense.

Specs that answer the collector

Case size, caliber, metal, stones, water resistance. ÉPINE ends on four numbers: 40 mm, 164 g on the bracelet, a 42-hour reserve and 50 m.

Gifting built in

Gift boxes, cards and wrapping as products of their own. Rouge No. 1 sells the same lipstick three ways: on its own, in its coffret and gift-wrapped.

Trust at a higher price

Shipping, returns, care and who's behind the brand, easy to find from every product. A big order needs more reassurance than a small one.

Options without a phone call

Metal, size or dial as options on the product, each with its own price and stock. ÉPINE offers four dials on the same watch.

03Questions

Questions before we start.

How much does a watch or jewelry website cost?

You get the exact price in writing before you pay. Once accepted, that price won't change for the agreed work. We'll quote any extra requests before starting them. It's mostly down to the number of pieces, options like metal, size or engraving, and the close-up imagery.

Can customers choose metal, size or engraving?

Yes. Metal, size and strap are options on the product, each with its own price and stock, and engraving can be a text field on the product page.

I already sell on Shopify. Do I have to leave it?

No. If you want your orders, stock and customers to stay in Shopify, we check your plan and setup first and, where it fits, Shopify keeps handling checkout. If you'd rather not pay for Shopify, the store runs on Stripe instead and the money goes straight to your account.

02What it takes

What a launch site has to get right.

A clear opening message

What it is and why it matters, before anything else. Ámbar opens on one line: a blood-orange serum for dark spots and uneven tone.

How it works, shown

The product turning, the parts up close, one at a time. ÉPINE takes the watch apart in order: the spikes, the crown, the dial.

Specs people can trust

A clean spec sheet with the numbers that matter. If it isn't measured, it's not there. ÉPINE closes on four: case, weight, power reserve and water resistance.

How to use it, in steps

A new product needs instructions people can follow on a phone. They won't read a manual. Ámbar gives three: clean skin, two or three drops, every morning before sunscreen.

Every launch state

Coming soon, on sale, sold out, next batch. The page is ready for each, so launch day is a switch and not a rebuild.

Light enough for launch day

Hand-written pages with nothing extra to load, so the rush from a launch post gets the product and not a blank screen.

03Questions

Questions before we start.

How much does a product launch website cost?

You get the exact price in writing before you pay. Once accepted, that price won't change for the agreed work. We'll quote any extra requests before starting them. It's mostly down to how much of the launch is animated and whether it'll take pre-orders.

Can we go live before stock is ready?

Yes. The site can open with an interest form and switch to selling when stock lands. Switching it over is a small change, priced before we make it.

Can you work from renders instead of photos?

Yes. Plenty of launches start from renders, and the site can swap to real photos whenever you have them.

I already sell on Shopify. Do I have to leave it?

No. If you want your orders, stock and customers to stay in Shopify, we check your plan and setup first and, where it fits, Shopify keeps handling checkout. If you'd rather not pay for Shopify, the store runs on Stripe instead and the money goes straight to your account.

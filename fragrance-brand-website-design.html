02What it takes

What a fragrance site has to get right.

The notes, readable in five seconds

Opening, heart and trace laid out so anyone can take them in at a glance. NACRE opens on sea salt and white tea, with mineral iris at the heart and soft sandalwood as the trace.

The bottle as the hero

Big, sharp images of the flacon, the cap and the glass. On a fragrance site the bottle does the job a model does on a fashion site.

Collections that don't turn into a catalog

Several scents, several sizes, discovery sets and samples, each with its own page and stock. SOLSTICE PARFUMS shows five extraits on one basalt wall: touch a niche and the bottle comes closer.

Atmosphere that still loads fast

Mood and motion that don't cost the visitor who tapped your link from a Reel. A heavy page loses them before the bottle shows up.

A clear path to payment

The bag and delivery steps are designed with the rest of the site. Payment runs on Stripe with your logo and colors, and the money goes straight to your account.

Launches you don't have to build

A new scent, a limited edition, a restock: you send it and we put it up, at a price you know before we start. Not a new project.

03Questions

Questions before we start.

How much does a perfume brand website cost?

You get the exact price in writing before you pay. Once accepted, that price won't change for the agreed work. We'll quote any extra requests before starting them. It's mostly down to the number of scents and sizes, discovery sets, and how much of the bottle's animated.

Can I sell discovery sets and samples?

Yes. Sets, samples and several sizes of the same scent are normal products in the store, each with its own price and stock.

Can I try a store before I decide?

Yes. NACRE and SOLSTICE PARFUMS both have a working bag: pick a perfume or a size, add it and watch the bag update. They're concepts, so no order is placed and no card is charged.

I already sell on Shopify. Do I have to leave it?

No. If you want your orders, stock and customers to stay in Shopify, we check your plan and setup first and, where it fits, Shopify keeps handling checkout. If you'd rather not pay for Shopify, the store runs on Stripe instead and the money goes straight to your account.

Before you start

Questions before we start

How much will my website cost?

You get the exact price in writing before you pay. Once accepted, that price won't change for the agreed work. We'll quote any extra requests before starting them.

How long will it take?

We'll agree a realistic schedule after checking your scope and materials, before you pay. You'll review the work and approve it before launch.

Are there monthly fees?

We'll list hosting, domain, maintenance and third-party costs separately in your quote. You'll see which recurring costs are required and which services are optional before you pay.

What if I need something extra?

We quote extra pages, content, features or revisions separately for your approval before doing the additional work.

Who owns the site?

You do. Once you pay the agreed project fee, the website design and code are yours. You can take your site with you.

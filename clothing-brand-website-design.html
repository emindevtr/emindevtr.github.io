02What it takes

What a clothing site has to get right.

Size and fit up front

A size picker, a size guide one tap away and the measurements that matter for that piece, beside the size picker.

Every colorway, properly

Pick a color and the photos change with it. Stock is kept per size and per color, so you never sell a jacket you don't have.

The fabric, close up

Material, weight and care, said plainly. In ZeroThread you turn the jacket, see the back and pinch in on the embroidery, so customers know what they're ordering.

Drops and limited runs

A launch date, a numbered run, a sold-out state that still looks good. SARP FOOTWEAR opens its Series 01 as ten silhouettes on one lit wall.

Lookbook and store in one

Editorial pages and product pages on the same site, instead of a pretty homepage that hands people off to a generic shop.

Bag and delivery in your style

The bag and delivery steps stay in your style. Payment runs on Stripe, or through Shopify if you already use it, with your logo and colors.

03Questions

Questions before we start.

How much does a clothing brand website cost?

You get the exact price in writing before you pay. Once accepted, that price won't change for the agreed work. We'll quote any extra requests before starting them. It's mostly down to the number of products, sizes and colorways, and the lookbook pages you'd like.

Can the store handle sizes, colors and stock?

Yes. Every product can have sizes and colors, each combination with its own stock and, if you want, its own photos and price.

Can you build a launch page for a drop?

Yes. A drop page with the date, the pieces, a numbered run and a sold-out state is a normal part of a clothing store we build.

I already sell on Shopify. Do I have to leave it?

No. If you want your orders, stock and customers to stay in Shopify, we check your plan and setup first and, where it fits, Shopify keeps handling checkout. If you'd rather not pay for Shopify, the store runs on Stripe instead and the money goes straight to your account.

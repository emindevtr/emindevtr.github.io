Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026 · Updated November 4, 2026

This policy describes how Emin Dev ("Emin Dev", "we", "us"), a freelance web design and development service run by Osman Emin, handles personal information. We keep it simple because we collect very little.

1. What we collect

  • Contact information you send us — if you email us, message us on social media or send a project enquiry, we receive your name, contact details and whatever you choose to tell us about your business.
  • Enquiry source — when you submit a form, we include campaign labels from the current page URL, if present, and the referring website hostname to understand where enquiries come from. We do not store these labels in cookies or browser storage.
  • Business information for your website — content you provide so we can build and maintain your site.
  • Basic server logs — like nearly every website, our hosting provider records standard technical data (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and reliability.

Anonymous website event counts

We count page loads and fixed actions such as viewing prices, opening the enquiry form and delivery errors. These aggregate counters contain no names, email addresses, message text, IP addresses, visitor identifiers or cookies. They measure actions, not unique people. Standard hosting logs remain separate.

Meta Pixel on our campaign page

Our campaign page (/brands) uses the Meta Pixel and Meta's Conversions API to measure how our ads on Facebook and Instagram perform. When you visit that page, Meta receives technical data such as your IP address, browser type, the page you are on, what you do there (for example, opening one of our sample sites or sending an enquiry) and a first-party cookie (_fbp) set by the pixel. If you send an enquiry from that page, we also send Meta a hashed (SHA-256) copy of your email address so it can tell which ad brought it; Hashing does not make your email anonymous: Meta may match it to an account. Meta processes this data under its own privacy policy.

Advertising measurement is optional in every country. Meta Pixel and Conversions API remain off unless you enable Advertising in our cookie settings. Analytics and advertising have separate choices. You can reject both and still use the website and enquiry form. Change or withdraw your choice at any time using Cookie settings.

The rest of this website sets no advertising cookies.

Google Analytics

Several pages of this website, including our home page and our campaign page, use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit, which pages they read, how long they stay, roughly where they are (country and city, worked out from the IP address, which Google Analytics does not store) and which ad or website brought them. It sets first-party cookies (_ga and _ga_*). Google processes this data under its own privacy policy; you can opt out with Google's opt-out add-on. Google Analytics remains off in every country until you enable Analytics in our cookie settings. We do not send earlier browsing actions after you consent. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using Cookie settings.

PostHog heatmaps

With the same Analytics permission, our pages use PostHog, hosted in the European Union, to see which parts of a page get clicked and how far people scroll (heatmaps). PostHog receives the page address without its query string (campaign labels excepted), the referring website, clicks on page elements, scroll depth, browser and device type and an approximate country. It does not record your screen, read what you type into forms, or keep your IP address (we have IP collection switched off). It stores a random identifier in a first-party cookie and browser storage (ph_*). PostHog processes this data under its own privacy policy. PostHog stays off until you enable Analytics, and withdrawing that choice deletes its cookie and storage on your device.

Remembering your cookie choice

Our first-party cookies asc_privacy_v, asc_analytics and asc_ads remember your settings for up to 180 days. The legacy asc_consent cookie is kept off for compatibility. These preference cookies do not identify you. Rejecting optional cookies does not prevent project enquiries. We respect Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals by keeping optional measurement off.

2. How we use it

  • To respond to you, discuss your project and deliver our services.
  • To invoice the services you order.
  • To keep our services secure and improve them.

We do not sell or rent personal information. Ever.

3. Who we share it with

Only service providers we need to operate: hosting infrastructure, payment processing (e.g. Stripe — we never see or store full card numbers), email, the messaging service used to notify us of enquiries, software tools (including AI services) that help us organise and answer messages, Meta Platforms for measuring our ads on the page described above, Google for Google Analytics, PostHog for heatmaps and Resend for the newsletter. Providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy policies. We limit what we disclose to the purpose described above.

4. Email

If we email your business about our services, every message identifies us, includes our postal address and contains a working way to opt out. Tell us to stop and we stop.

Newsletter

Our newsletter is separate from project enquiries: asking for a quote or buying from us does not subscribe you. If you enter your email in the newsletter form, we send one email asking you to confirm. You are only added after you confirm, and if you don't, the request is discarded. The newsletter covers new websites, project news and occasional offers, no more than once a month.

To prove your consent we keep, with your email address, when you asked, when you confirmed, the page you used and the version of the text you saw. We use Resend to send these emails and store the subscriber list; Resend may record whether an email was delivered, opened or clicked. Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link and one-click unsubscribe. After you unsubscribe we keep only your address marked as unsubscribed, so we never email you again by mistake; ask us and we will delete it.

5. Retention

We keep client information for as long as you are a client and as long as required for tax and legal records afterwards. Prospect information we delete on request. Newsletter subscriptions last until you unsubscribe; unconfirmed requests are not stored.

6. Your rights

You can ask us at any time what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to oeminsisman@gmail.com and we will respond promptly.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland: we process personal data on the legal bases of contract performance (delivering the services you request) and legitimate interest (responding to enquiries, keeping our services secure), and your consent for the Meta Pixel, Google Analytics, PostHog and the newsletter. You additionally have the rights to data portability, to restrict or object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. We and our service providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States; where required, we rely on our providers' standard contractual clauses for such transfers.
